Initializing Origin...

My descent into cybersecurity started not with malicious intent, but with an insatiable curiosity about how things break. At 14, I successfully bypassed the network restrictions at my high school using a DNS tunnel. Instead of exploiting it, I wrote a detailed report for the IT admin.

Since then, that curiosity has evolved into a disciplined pursuit. From reverse-engineering malware in a home lab to participating in global CTF tournaments, my focus has shifted from finding the breaks to designing the fixes. I am passionate about DevSecOps, applied cryptography, and protecting critical infrastructure.
